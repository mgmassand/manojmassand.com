About

Working where medicine, technology, and business strategy meet.

Manoj Massand is a board-certified neuroradiologist and a partner at Southwest Neuro-Imaging at Barrow Neurological Institute in Phoenix. His career has run through three of the strongest imaging environments in American medicine: Barrow, Cleveland Clinic, and Baylor University Medical Center.

In 2026 he completed an MBA with Honors at The Wharton School (University of Pennsylvania), with majors in Finance, Business Analytics, and Artificial Intelligence for Business. He puts his clinical and business training to work on bringing AI into practice, running imaging operations efficiently, and making the financial case for both.

Prior to medical school, he studied chemical engineering at Princeton graduating Phi Beta Kappa and Magna Cum Laude. A competitive tennis player, he captained the varsity team at Deerfield Academy and played junior varsity tennis at Princeton, and he still plays racket sports and golf. He is also a distance runner with 30 marathons behind him, including 11 Boston Marathon finishes, and a personal record of 2:59:55.

Focus

Four areas where clinical judgment and business training apply together.

AI strategy and clinical integration

Deciding which AI tools earn a place in the reading room, validating them against real cases, and fitting them into the workflow so they change turnaround and triage rather than sit unused.

Imaging operations and enterprise value

Scheduling, technologist workflow, pricing, and professional productivity across multi-site imaging enterprises, seen from both an academic health system and a physician-owned practice.

Healthcare finance and analytics

Forecasting, valuation, and business analytics applied to practice management, growth, and investment decisions in imaging and the wider health system.

Physician leadership and education

Building teams and leading change among physicians, grounded in nine years directing fellowship training and two decades teaching residents and fellows.
